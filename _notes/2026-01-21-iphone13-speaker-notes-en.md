## Summary
An iPhone 13 with a completely dead bottom speaker after a mechanical impact.  
The failure was caused by an internal open circuit inside the loudspeaker module.

## Key symptoms
- no sound from the bottom speaker;
- top speaker working normally;
- no visible corrosion or debris;
- issue appeared after a drop.

## Diagnostics highlights
- speaker tested with a multimeter showed an open circuit;
- cleaning alone did not restore sound;
- audio channels tested separately confirmed hardware failure;
- impact location correlated with the damaged module.

## Technical notes
- the loudspeaker voice coil uses extremely thin copper wires;
- mechanical shock can break the coil without external damage;
- compressed air should never be used near a connected microphone;
- replacement is the only reliable fix once the coil is broken.

## Practical conclusion
After drops affecting the lower frame, speaker failure is often mechanical, not contamination-related.  
Electrical testing is essential before attempting cleaning or partial fixes.
