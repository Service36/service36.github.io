## Case goal
Battery replacement requested mainly because the phone was being handed over to another user. The objective was predictable battery life and a clean, non-alarming system status after repair.

## Intake snapshot (old battery)
- Reported health: ~83%
- Charge cycles: 518
- Manufacturer: Sunwoda

Interpretation: not a dead battery, but 500+ cycles is meaningful mileage. Replacement makes sense for handover use cases.

## Options discussed (pricing includes labor)
1) Budget replacement - 3500 RUB  
   - Works normally, but iOS may show an "Unable to verify" authenticity warning.

2) "Paired" battery workflow - 4200 RUB (chosen)  
   - Finalized via Repair Assistant (service mode).
   - Settings show battery health at 100%.
   - No authenticity warning after completion.
   - Parts & Service History may show status as "Used" (system label nuance).

3) AASP route - 9200 RUB  
   - Apple Authorized Service Provider path (most "official" outcome).

## Repair flow (high-level)
- Open device carefully
- Remove old battery and prep seating area
- Install paired battery
- Run Repair Assistant in service mode to complete the repair
- Re-seal with fresh adhesive (primer + heat + pressure)
- Post-checks (basic functions + charging behavior)

## Post-repair checks
- Battery status: 100% in Settings, no "Unable to verify" message
- Charging: peak current observed up to ~2.5A during fast charge phase (then tapering as expected)

## Output
- Time: ~50 minutes (appointment slot)
- Price: 4200 RUB
- Warranty: 3 months
- Paperwork: job completion document + fiscal receipt
