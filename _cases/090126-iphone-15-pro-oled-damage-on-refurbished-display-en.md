## Summary
Local OLED damage on an iPhone 15 Pro caused by pressure on a refurbished display.  
The device was a modified US model converted to physical SIM.

## Key symptoms
- Black spot on the display without glass cracks
- Instant defect after applying screen protector
- Normal device operation except affected display area
- No signs of drop or external impact

## Diagnostics highlights
- Visual inspection revealed a physical SIM tray
- Internal inspection confirmed SIM conversion
- Display module showed signs of glass replacement
- Damage pattern consistent with point pressure on OLED

## Technical notes
- Refurbished displays have lower structural rigidity
- Non-original frames increase stress concentration
- SIM-converted devices often use custom batteries
- Factory water resistance cannot be restored

## Practical conclusion
OLED damage without visible glass break often indicates a refurbished display.  
Independent diagnostics are critical when dealing with secondary market devices.
